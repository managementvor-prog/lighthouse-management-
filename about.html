Our Story

Two decades of guiding exceptional talent to extraordinary heights in entertainment

A Legacy of Excellence

Founded in 2004, Lighthouse Management emerged from a singular vision: to provide world-class talent with representation that matches their caliber. What began as a boutique agency in Beverly Hills has grown into one of the entertainment industry's most respected names.

Our philosophy is simple — every client deserves unwavering dedication, strategic guidance, and access to opportunities that align with their unique brand and aspirations. We don't just manage careers; we cultivate legacies.

From negotiating landmark deals to navigating the evolving landscape of streaming, social media, and global markets, our team brings decades of combined expertise to every partnership.
