A Legacy of Excellence

Founded in 2004, Lighthouse Management emerged from a simple belief: that every extraordinary talent deserves representation that matches their ambition. What began as a boutique agency in Beverly Hills has grown into one of the most respected names in global entertainment.

Our team of seasoned agents, lawyers, and brand strategists work in unison to navigate the complex landscape of modern entertainment. From negotiating landmark film deals to building billion-dollar brand partnerships, we operate at the intersection of art and commerce.
